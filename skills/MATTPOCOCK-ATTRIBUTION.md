The PR summary-visual menu in `skills/pr-review/SKILL.md` originates in the
`show-me` skill of [humanlayer/skills](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills)
by Dex Horthy (MIT), reached through Matt Pocock's `pr` skill.

Local modifications adapt the text for opencode (skills are always
model-reachable, so user-invoked flows became commands), drop the upstream
issue-tracker, label, and GLOSSARY/ADR conventions, and fold the material into
existing skills where the triggers overlapped.
